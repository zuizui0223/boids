# Same geographical space, different ecological states: v7 novelty and independent-source audit

**Date:** 2026-11-15. **Status:** retrospective literature audit + no-outcome source preflight. **No new field-animal effect**, no revised existing frozen endpoint. This document supersedes *claims about novelty*, not measured results, in boids PR #146.

## Research decision

The four-system comparative manuscript in [louis PR #7](https://github.com/zuizui0223/louis/pull/7), *Shared places, different ecological lives*, is an **ecological comparative synthesis / perspective-like narrative**, **not** a new pooled cross-taxon original research demonstration. Its axes have different measurement and process definitions: Serengeti conditional activity detections; bat repeated height use; King Rail matched available-vs-used water depth; California waterfowl categorical successions. Do not pool the corresponding information, state-buffering, or transition effects; the same datasets contribute to other manuscripts.

A distinct already-written original ecology manuscript in the same louis PR #7, *Twilight reorganization of winter habitat use*, offers a narrower **exploratory, single-source observational reanalysis**: 329 USGS California bird-year sequences, 56,591 adjacent approximately hourly moving-step destination pairs, 9,586 crossing source day/night. The lesser snow goose (`LSGO`) conditioned boundary-switch excess is **+8.4721 percentage points**, candidate-ID bootstrap 95% **[+5.7221,+11.1619]**, **54/69** estimable candidate tags positive. White-fronted geese, northern pintail and mallard did *not* meet the analogous criterion. A **post-result** directional enrichment in sequential **destination classes** `Protected → Rice` is LSGO **+32.3608 pp [22.1254, 43.3262]**, northern pintail **+55.5443 pp [37.5350, 72.7672]**; reverse `Rice → Protected` did not show consistent enhancement. These are **successive recorded destination categories**, not validated roost-to-rice flights, directly observed foraging or a causal light treatment. Source `Protected` is land protection, and source one-hot assumptions/exclusions must be audited against provider definitions. Candidate tag-prefix independence remains unresolved. Numerical provenance: original source receipts in louis PR #7, not new boids analysis.

### Existing failure evidence retained, not turned into universal ecology

- boids PR #146 King Rail v5, 10 bird-year matched-event sequences: current event vs previous-three-date contextual predictor **+9.8664 cm²** average squared-error gain with interval **[-1.9004,+31.3281]**, 5/10 positive (frozen **FAIL**). v5 confounds timing and smoothing.
- Marais Breton v4, 17 independent sites / 67 annual transitions: extra annual hydrological information has uncertain heldout improvement **+0.007548 nats** with interval spanning zero; no individual choices.
- USGS California managed-wetlands v6e, 36 held-out properties: adding same-visit water-depth CV to fixed mean-depth/landscape predictors worsens original shorebird-vs-dabbling log-count contrast MSE **-0.035539** (95% property bootstrap [-0.056213,-0.017547], 15/36 positive). This does not refute an observed within-property habitat association or demonstrate absence of an accessible state portfolio.
- King Rail two random 75-m plots per event do not identify true accessible preferred-state proportion K; louis PR #6 constructs both continuous no-K=0 and discrete positive-mass-at-K=0 worlds consistent with the same two-plot counts. Therefore **NO measured reachable-state exhaustion threshold** exists.

## Sharper falsifiable prospect: context-dependent state-transition *surplus*

Use independent GPS tracking with a verified true movement origin class `H_start`, actual destination `H_used`, time-matched accessible potential destinations `H_avail,j`, and independent animal IDs. Do **not** substitute consecutive recorded endpoints from source-filtered moving observations for a verified physical origin, and do not equate protected status with exclusive habitat class without a documented provider crosswalk.

For animal `i`, decision step `s`, define (for exclusive classes only):

    e_is = 1[H_used != H_start] - sum_j w_js * 1[H_avail,j != H_start]

**Primary proposed comparison**: excess switching at dusk/day-night transitions versus matched within-photoperiod times, while controlling original season, prior state, movement length, individual and local alternative states. Split whole **animals and study areas**, not GPS fixes; report source identity overlap and multi-year tracking duplication. A `PASS` requires positive site- or population-heldout group evidence (not just a within-original-source candidate-ID interval), and meaningful source-defined actual origin/availability before outcomes are read. Under this design, identifying which species differ in **boundary timing beyond the actually accessible state distribution** would be a more defensible original ecology test. Without these data, manuscript belongs to single-source exploratory original work or ecological synthesis, respectively.

### Candidate independent source: USGS Matchett et al. (2021), DOI 10.5066/P922KDU6

The official source explicitly relates *primary roosts to night feeding locations* and dynamic open-water maps in California winters 2015–2018. ScienceBase parent item `601478d0d34edf5c66ea0ead`. It may contain actual roost/feeding locations and time-matched flooding layers, but **publication metadata alone is insufficient to establish** individual event origin, a source-defined matched endpoint join, or an unobserved set of physically reachable alternate locations. It may overlap geographically and biologically with the Overton/Casazza waterfowl work (same coauthors, overlapping years); an identity/collection crosswalk is required before declaring source independence. Source-only inventory `comparative_state/usgs_roost_source_v7.py` must stop unless metadata and original downloads establish a defensible route. Do not score a positive behavioral effect from a title or aggregate distance column.

**Next experiment should be independent physical movement data or a registered prospective water-management contrast; not more posthoc models on the same boids/louis sources.**
