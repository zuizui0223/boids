# USGS primary sanctuary / waterfowl v7e original-source structural stop

Date: 2026-11-15. The source is official ScienceBase under DOI 10.5066/P922KDU6. This note preserves a **source-structure finding**, not an ecological null or positive field effect.

All three original CSVs were fetched from the exact publisher `downloadUri` and matched their publisher MD5 bytes. SHA256 and first-row headers were recorded in `comparative_state/results/usgs_roost_csv_schema_v7e_original_receipt.json`; no row values, individual IDs, site positions, or observed outcomes were opened for ecological scoring. Historical v7d URL-404 stop remains intact and is explained by previously *guessed* URLs.

Original **distance** CSV (5,258,934 bytes) has only `Region, Year class, Month class, Distance to primary sanctuaries (km)`. It does **not** establish identifiable GPS movement-step start/end, a roost-to-feeding linked dyad, bird ID, or matched available destinations. Distance to a primary sanctuary is **not** physical displacement along an animal's observed path.

Original **wet habitat** CSV (3,195,785 bytes) has only `Species, Direct open water, Adjacent area, Adjacent area post-shrinkage`; these are categorical habitat metrics, not timestamped per-individual telemetry points. **Flooding area** CSV (5,014 bytes) has only `Open-water raster mosaic list, Direct open water, Adjacent area, Adjacent area post-shrinkage` — its mosaic-name context does not supply a GPS-to-habitat-time join.

Therefore the prespecified original primary endpoint `I(destination-state differs from actual step-origin) - availability-matched switching probability` is **not identifiable from these files**. No cross-source estimate of habitat choice, accessible-state portfolio exhaustion, physical roost-to-feeding route, or independent California waterfowl replication should be claimed.

Publication allocation: keep broader `Shared places, different ecological lives` as ecological **comparative synthesis** (nonpooled, overlapping sources) and keep the narrower single-USGS-source category-ordering manuscript in louis PR #7 as **exploratory observational original reanalysis**, pending nonexclusive habitat/protection variable audit and original tag identity checks. Published 2023 VonBank goose transition models, 2025 Ramos microclimate-refugia tracking, and 2026 Franke cross-species iSSA already constrain generic novelty claims. For an independent original test seek truly timestamped GPS origins and destinations plus matched reachable alternatives and spatial/environment context, before selecting source based on positive effects.
