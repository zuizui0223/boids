# v8 — independent individual-level state-transition test (FROZEN BEFORE OUTCOME ACCESS)

Date: 2026-11-16; state: **source-only preflight**. The existing boids PR #146 and louis PR #7 remain separately frozen. No animal or land-cover outcomes are opened in this version.

## Source choice

**Primary**: Handel, Spiegel & Shwartz (2025), DOI `10.5061/dryad.wwpzgmsxg`, published in *Journal of Applied Ecology* DOI `10.1111/1365-2664.70120`. The Dryad README describes 68 tagged white-spectacled bulbuls at 4–8 s cadence and 64 experimental translocations, with `iSSA.data.list.RData` containing individual ID, `step_id_`, `t1_`, `t2_`, `case_`, movement metrics, actual step origin (`x1_,y1_`,`landUse_start`) and endpoint (`x2_,y2_`,`landUse_end`) plus identically structured simulated available steps. Its original paper already established the value of vegetated field margins as movement corridors and species avoidance of arable fields. **Do not claim those findings as novel.**

**Backup source-only**: USGS 2022 DOI `10.5066/P9XBZKZ8`, GPS locations with observed water conditions and annotated daily life-history for dabbling ducks. This has potential overlap with existing USGS cohorts and lacks matched random destination states in the published description; it is **not** substitute for the primary matched-step source.

## Scientific question

When animals move within the same agricultural landscape, does *origin-state dependence* remain after controlling destination land-use selection and ordinary locomotor costs? A bird's land-use transition is not established merely by presence in vegetation or by marginal habitat preference. In the model comparison, held-out individuals must be better predicted when one conditions on **origin × destination** than by the destination alone, with the **same source-constructed accessible step alternatives**.

### Conditional-choice model and safeguards

For true step s from individual i, the frozen candidate set C(s) consists of exactly 1 original used step and its source-generated available alternatives with exactly the same original `id + burst_ + step_id_ + t1_ + t2_` metadata and start location/state. All steps must have: one and only one used candidate; at least 2 source alternatives; finite step length/turn; unambiguous single exclusive land-use state at origin and destination. If the source class codes/available rows do not match these conditions, **STOP** rather than infer from row order. Do not treat candidate endpoints as independent animals.

Compare model M0 (training-only origin-independent destination-class effects + log step length + cos turn angle, with ridge shrinkage) and M1 (same plus 1[origin land-use = destination land-use] *or*, if prespecified categories permit, origin-class × destination-class interactions). Because original study focused on movement-mode-only tracks and land-use choice, model M1's prediction contrast is an ecological **transition organization** test, not direct proof of memory, foraging or causal corridor connectivity. The final category roster, encoding and model regularization must be frozen after source-only header/schema but before seeing choice labels joined to land cover.

**Primary estimand**: for each held-out bird i, `Gain_i = logloss_i(M0) − logloss_i(M1)` on the exact same source-matched candidate sets; mean equal-bird held-out log-score gain with 95% bird-resampled CI. Whole-individual 5-fold split with stable SHA256 IDs, source identities never trained in their own holdout fold. Minimum reliable scientific admission 20 independently identified birds each with 30 valid steps, 2000 total matched steps, >=3 exclusive origin/destination categories, and >=2 source alternatives per matched step. Preserve negative gains and sparse classes. If IDs/timestamps differ only due to resampling or translocation, audit and report original trial-specific dependence; do not count the same bird twice.

**Secondary (descriptive) metric**: per origin category, actual **retention excess** `1[dest=origin] − mean_j 1[available_j=origin]`. This is a feature of source-defined sampled availability, not true occupancy of all reachable habitat. Zero sampled alternatives does not imply a state is inaccessible (two-plot K counterexample in louis PR #6). No post-hoc choice of 'favored' origin category or margin width based on effect sign.

### Independent validation / biological meaning

Compared with the existing California USGS waterfowl results, this source is a different taxon, landscape, tracking technology, original observer system, and measured state dimension. It can provide **independent taxon/system comparison** if original download and ID gates pass, not replication of hydrological or diel outcomes. Require out-of-individual performance and uncertainty. Do not pool its log-score with ODSP Serengeti mutual information or King Rail variance buffering.

Research novelty is **incremental origin-conditioned state organization under matched alternatives**, not time-of-day switching, habitat selection, or habitat refugia. If M1 fails in holdout, it is valid evidence that this dataset does not support transferable origin-conditioned behavior beyond baseline; it does not disprove all state tracking. The source belongs to one landscape, so regional or species universality cannot be inferred regardless of sign.

## Pre-source-opening numerical model freeze (2026-11-16 v8b)

The runnable known-truth learner ``matched_state_learner_v8.py`` adds a single **binary origin-retention indicator** (same categorical origin and candidate destination) in M1. **Origin × destination full interactions are not opened** and are not an alternative primary. M0 contains all source-declared exclusive destination-class fixed effects (first lexicographically sorted class is the omitted reference), standardized log step length, and cosine turn angle. Five whole-individual folds are assigned by SHA256(animal ID) mod 5, without target labels in the fold split. Continuous feature moments come from training candidates alone. Each bird's total training contribution is exactly equal, regardless of step counts. Fit each conditional softmax model with L2 penalty 0.02 and L-BFGS-B (180 max iterations). Primary outcome is animal-equally weighted held-out log score M0 minus M1, in nats per actual used choice among the source alternatives, with 10,000 animal-bootstrap replicates, numpy RNG seed 20261116. PASS if lower 95% animal-bootstrap percentile >0 AND >=60% heldout birds have positive gain; otherwise FAIL. Do not tune thresholds, classes, split assignments, ridge, or covariate definitions after opening source-matched selection labels. Minimum 20 eligible birds, >=30 eligible steps/bird, >=2,000 complete steps total, >=3 mutually exclusive land-use classes, one actual used and >=2 alternatives per source-defined choice set. Group key is exact original animal, burst, step, start and end time; stop for incomplete/discordant groups. The source-specific RData-to-flat-case adapter is *not* considered valid until original SHA256 has been checked and class schema verified. This is a **prospective methodological design**, not an observed effect.

**Final schema/identifiability safeguards:** The original exact x1/y1 of every candidate in a source-defined step must agree within 1 m, with finite original x1/y1/x2/y2. At least two distinct origin land-use classes must each be represented by >=5 birds and >=100 source-defined step sets. Otherwise the same-state regressor can be confounded with a destination preference and the biological gate is STOP. These rules were fixed before reading any movement outcomes.
