## Exactly three models (no model search after held-out outcome)

Within each leave-one-bat-out fold (one physical bat's ALL original source rows withheld; learn from other original bats only), fit:

1. `M0` constant source capture rate `lambda0 = (Y_train + .5)/(E_train + 1)`, expected count `mu_i=E_i*lambda0` for every held-out row. The .5/1 fixed pseudocount pair is an explicit non-empirical smoothing convention for numeric stability.
2. `M1` direct density model, Poisson log rate `lambdaY(z)=exp(beta0+beta1*z+beta2*z^2)`, weighted by offset `log(E)`, L2 penalty `0.5*0.1*(beta1^2+beta2^2)`, intercept unpenalized. Fit `scipy.optimize.minimize(L-BFGS-B)` with analytic gradients and locked optimization tolerance. The quadratic is **prior-art hypothesis** (original study found capture rate peaks at moderate social signal density), not discovered here.
3. `M2` mechanistic bookkeeping (not independently causal): attack attempt rate `lambdaA(z)=exp(alpha0+alpha1*z)`, source attack count Poisson with offset `E`; and `pSuccess(z)=logistic(gamma0+gamma1*z)`, conditional success `Y|A~Binomial(A,p)`. Penalize nonintercepts at same 0.1 L2. Combined expected successful captures `mu_i=E_i*lambdaA(z)*pSuccess(z)`. Same training bats, fixed covariate z and identical held-out data as M1. **Decomposition is a statistical factorization of source counts, not a causal determination of cooperation versus interference.**

Scientific primary evaluation = **bat-equal mean** of held-out **Poisson deviance for observed captured prey counts Y** (or equivalent log-score; report exact deviance per source 5s exposure to account for different grouped rows), and two predeclared bat-level paired differences `D1 = score(M1)-score(M0)`, `D2 = score(M2)-score(M0)`. Negative = out-of-bat predictive improvement. Secondary `D21=M2-M1`, catch-count absolute error/exposure, calibration of total captures, and separate held-out attack count deviance and conditional successful/attack log-loss under M2. Report all folds, n_bats, original 5s exposure, source group size/distribution and bat-weighted mean. **No source row-level statistical p-value.** Bootstrap bat (10) 5000 draws seed=20261105 for descriptive paired CI; no invented independence of source group/time.

Mandatory guardrails: if positive exposure is absent or model fit fails/nonfinite, STOP full source inference; do not add response-driven cutoffs. Predeclared diagnostics of source density coverage (0, (0,25], (25,75], >75 conspecific CALLS per 5s, mirroring published categories) and per-bat share of source exposure, to identify unsupported high-density response. At the published 75-call threshold, report observed source supports by bat **without** turning prior-art density threshold into novel discovery. Report no unseen original-bat test if any leave-one-bat-out fold has fewer than 7 training bats.

Original `sum_commute`, `sum_disrupt` are retained as original source field quality summaries ONLY; do not invent what counts mean without publisher source documentation, and do not retrospectively drop rows according to those outcomes. No attempt to infer observed bat spacing in physical metres from number of other-bat calls. No new sound recordings, fieldwork or source original-file redistribution.
