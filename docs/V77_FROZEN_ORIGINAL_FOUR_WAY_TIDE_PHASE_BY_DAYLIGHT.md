## One primary tide-focused interaction, stronger matching than v76

Question: When comparing **the very same individual in the same calendar month and the same 0.20m measured waterheight band**, is the nighttime-minus-daytime positional preference for a native *modeled monthly tidal exposure spatial index* different when the actual measured RWS water is RISING (flood) vs FALLING (ebb)?

The directly testable four-way difference-in-differences for each fully observed original GPS bird `i`, month `m`, original physically measured height band `h` is:

[
D_{imh}=\left[\overline{r}_{night,flood}-\overline{r}_{day,flood}\right]_{imh}
-\left[\overline{r}_{night,ebb}-\overline{r}_{day,ebb}\right]_{imh}.
]

`r` is the same observed GPS used-location original NIOZ 1km native model exposure-index midrank as v75/v76, numeric unit UNKNOWN, not foraging/exposed hours. A positive primary `D` means **the night−day difference in *modeled tidal index use* was higher in flood than ebb when original individual/month/current waterheight are shared**; it does NOT prove phase-dependent night feeding, causal dynamics, animal clock anticipation or true position-specific intertidal emergence.

## Important inferential limit and why this is still about tide

This checks whether tidal **rising/falling direction modulates spatial habitat use differently by daylight** at the *same* observed tide height in a *single* original 2010 Schier oystercatcher cohort. The NIOZ `time_expo` field has *unknown physical unit* and **monthly mean**, not actual hour-by-hour patch exposure; source GPS locations could reflect roosting rather than feeding, and unmeasured coastal habitat/geomorphology/accessibility/individual sampling times remain. The difference-in-differences is an **associational four-way contrast**, not proof of tidal steering, animal internal clocks, phase-dependent prey field, or social Reynolds/Boids alignment. **The interaction direction was suggested by opening v76** and is not independent confirmation or species universality.
