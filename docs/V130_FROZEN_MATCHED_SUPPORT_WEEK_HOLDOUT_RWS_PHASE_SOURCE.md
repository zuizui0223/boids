## Fixed forecast models and five-block CV (no hyperparameter selection)

Hold out ISO calendar week modulo 5 (`utc_event.isocalendar().week % 5`), **all birds together**, training on other four folds. All original 2018 held-out weeks are from the same source site and many same birds appear in training — forecast is **within-source temporal transport, not independent site/species/bird**. Require each fold contain >=2 distinct weeks and >=3 tagged birds.

Target: per-original-bird×15min ACC forage classifier event share `Y` in [0,1]. Use fixed Ridge least squares with intercept unpenalized and identical lambda **20.0** for all non-intercept coefficients, fixed raw original predictor scales and no outcomes-based model selection. Report clipped 0..1 heldout predictions and squared loss, not log-odds/feeding minutes.

All models include fixed observed-source feature bases:
- **CLOCK**: intercept, genuine tagged-bird dummies (alphabetically sorted private IDs, one omitted), month dummies Jul/Aug/Sep, UTC time-of-day sine/cosine harmonics k=1,2,3 for 24h and within-month centered calendar-day linear term ((day−15.5)/15).
- **SOLAR**: CLOCK plus true original GPS astronomical night indicator (1 night, 0 day).
- **TIDE-0**: SOLAR plus 5 fixed predictors from real observed 2018 gauge: source water height in metres NAP, rising/falling phase ±1, height×phase, night×phase, night×height.
- **TIDE-3**, **TIDE-7**, **TIDE-11**: exactly the same five tide predictors, but from original truly observed gauge shifted +3,+7,+11d respectively. Same number of coefficients, same folds, same original actual GPS/ACC Y and solar observations; **only tide alignment changes**. Date/time clock and source sample stay unchanged.

CV score: within each source UTC week, average bin-level squared errors per original bird, then average equally across genuine birds with at least one test row in week, then equally across the independent calendar weeks. Weeks are the **score unit**, not 200k independent sensor bins. Report score per model, number of source weeks, birds, 5 fold counts, and percentage of source weeks with positive paired improvement, plus five per-fold predictions aggregate (not any raw timestamps).

Prespecified exploratory contrasts (MSE lower is better): `D_solar = MSE_CLOCK−MSE_SOLAR`; `D_tide0 = MSE_SOLAR−MSE_TIDE-0`; `D_ghost[k] = MSE_SOLAR−MSE_TIDE-k`; `D_phase = D_tide0 − max(D_ghost[3],D_ghost[7],D_ghost[11])`. Also report each paired real-vs-ghost week score. A positive `D_phase` is necessary but not sufficient for real phase specificity. All scores are measured out-of-week, unlike the in-sample original v129 I. If solar doesn't outperform CLOCK, retain previous solar **association** but explicitly no incremental light-vs-clock predictive information.

Fixed 10,000 **whole-ISO-week** paired nonparametric bootstrap (seed 20261114) of held-out week score vectors, replaying identical week indices across models; report percentile 95% of `D_solar`, `D_tide0` and `D_phase`. These conditional CV-bootstrap intervals do NOT account for full model-refit uncertainty or unique independent environmental years, and are **exploratory descriptive intervals, not causal significance tests**. Positive phase contrast with lower CI >0 and >=60% positive individual real-minus-best-ghost weeks would be suggestive *within source only*; otherwise phase uniqueness **NOT ESTABLISHED**. Even a positive signal cannot demonstrate local dry-flat exposure: harbour tide within 25km remains a proxy without local DEM and feeding observations.
