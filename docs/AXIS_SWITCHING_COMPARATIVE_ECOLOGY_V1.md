# From individual differences to ecological axis switching

**31 October 2026 — PROSPECTIVE HYPOTHESIS / CROSS-REPOSITORY EVIDENCE AUDIT.**
Not a new empirical cross-taxon confirmation; not an approved manuscript or preregistered independent experiment. Do not reopen frozen JAE or claim new source outcomes.

## The research question that is actually general

**When ecological opportunities change, which dimension absorbs the change: location, behavioral use within location, experienced resource state, or population-level occupancy/concentration?**

This is sharper than "individual niches are repeatable and overlap." In 2026 the general existence of individualised niches and overlap is already well reviewed (Krüger et al., Biological Reviews, doi:10.1002/brv.70147); ecological niche choice/conformance has established prior art (doi:10.1093/biosci/biac023, verified against the 2022 published BioScience article). The novelty target is **prospectively predicting an axis switch from independently measured accessible opportunities across genuinely independent animal systems**, rather than baptizing descriptive examples as a universal law.

## Candidate hypothesis — opportunity-gated axis switching (UNTESTED)

An animal can maintain a similar ecological *state* by changing *place*, or maintain a similar *place* by changing *activity, height, timing or local resource use*. When the pool of accessible viable options shrinks, both kinds of compensation may fail, potentially yielding broader relocation, concentration on remaining suitable patches, or abandonment.

**Crucial conditional structure, not a universal sign claim:**

- **Local opportunity high:** a perturbed individual can shift locally while retaining exposure to a favored environmental state. Prediction: low change in experienced environmental state relative to local time-matched availability, despite measurable changes in chosen locations **if paired spatial data exist**. Persistent different within-place behavioral policies may coexist with high geographic overlap.
- **Local opportunity low; regional alternatives reachable:** predict increased relocation outside the original local availability region, with some state tracking if the regional options are suitable.
- **Local and regional opportunity low:** predict poor state tracking, concentration where suitable patches remain, abandonment or demographic loss. Which response occurs depends on colonial central-place constraints, density/capacity and mobility; **not every system must concentrate**.
- **No meaningful perturbation or inaccessible alternatives:** the hypothesis does not predict a forced mode change.

These pathways are *candidate mechanisms*, not three already observed stages of one animal lineage. Competition, morphology, seasonality and social information are competing explanations/covariates, not declared absent.

## Independent variables and observational units

Define before accessing target outcomes:
- **O_local(t), O_regional(t):** available independently mapped and *accessible* ecological options under a defined movement cost, measured from external water depth, snow/vegetation, independent resource/phenology surveys, habitat or breeding-substrate capacity. Must not be counted from the animals' chosen positions or a future-leaking pseudo-availability kernel.
- **D_X:** change in each animal's spatial use distribution, with source-appropriate position error, resolution curves and opportunity-matched dates.
- **D_E:** change in independently characterized *experienced* environmental state (water depth, habitat, real diet if measured), benchmarked against time-matched available alternatives.
- **D_B:** change in conditionally measured behavior/time/height at common places, where sensing supports the axis.
- **R:** same-individual vs exchangeable-conspecific held-out predictive advantage with sample-size/estimator null calibration; use an equal-record control if model training information differs.
- **C:** population-level effective site/component number for colonial breeding systems, with fixed-composition count and observation null, **an independent hierarchical response, never pooled as an individual R/X observation**.

The central prediction is that **opportunity predicts the *type* of change and its measurable out-of-source outcome**, not that one raw overlap metric should correlate with individuality in every species.

A prospective schematic model is:
`P(response mode | ΔO_local, ΔO_regional, mobility, anchoring, condition, prior individual state)`.
The response mode is determined using independent tracked state/place changes, not assigned after inspecting which correlation is strongest. Model a multinomial/cause-specific response only if all categories and event windows are estimable; otherwise use separately frozen continuous D_X and D_E tests with honest missingness.

## First next scientific decision (do not expand every repo)

**Primary target:** get a source whose original **same individuals × before/after environmental change** rows jointly contain genuine place, independently measured available and used ecological state, and behavioral opportunity. The Lake Erie rail source already measures availability but lacks certified event-to-coordinate linkage; its missing link is a genuine structural gap. A large multi-species mammalian iSSA source describes used/available choice sets but original 1.8-GB data require separate source and rights admission. A penguin breeding-site atlas could test a separate population-level downstream implication but is not a substitute for individual movement evidence.

**Minimum sequence:**

1. Build a source-eligibility matrix from existing public-source inventories with a hard YES/NO/MISSING for verified biological individual/colony, before/after support, real independently measured opportunity, real used state, location, sensor error, rights and independence. Do not use DOI count as population n.
2. Identify one admissible system in a **different clade** with both spatial and resource-state change; lock scientific definitions before examining its response. Prefer a source not previously mined to select a favorable result.
3. Compare opportunity-aware prediction with **equal-training-information** and availability-blind baselines in whole-individual, future-season holdouts. Source/study-level external holdout before fitting any universal effect; keep negative sources.
4. Only after at least **three major clades and multiple independent cohorts** with the **same primary estimand**, evaluate between-clade heterogeneity; measure opportunity × axis response as an effect with source- and phylogeny-aware uncertainty. No arbitrary all-positive rule or post-outcome cutoff.
5. If source eligibility fails, close this as a candidate theory/feasibility result, not a demonstrated general ecological finding.

**Important falsifiers:** comparable environmental opportunity does not predict the type of response beyond movement opportunity/measurement alone; apparent axis switching vanishes with matched subjects/time and independently quantified resource availability; predictions fail on untouched populations or a new clade.

Freeze article packaging changes as a priority. PR #99 and JAE rc2 remain scientific record/possible narrow-field publication routes, but cannot be transformed into general ecological laws by title changes, combining unrelated p values or mixing population-colony and individual data.
