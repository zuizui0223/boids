# v11 frozen same-scene source: wild individual movement and localized fish calls

**New source finding (verified publisher paper before v11 original video read)**: Mouy et al. 2023 *Methods in Ecology and Evolution*, DOI [10.1111/2041-210X.14095](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.14095), published original [OSF q8dz4](https://osf.io/q8dz4), used genuinely contemporaneous underwater **videos, hydrophone waveforms and 3D acoustic source localizations**. Figure9 shows a moving lingcod over ~10s; Figure10 shows a lingcod and *three quillback rockfish* interacting in a 17s scene; authors matched sounds to rockfish using source positions and manually inspected video (already published fact). This is not a generic chorusing fish-school dataset and source labels do not provide all individual fish trajectories.
