## Main new question: when is tide-generated space available in sunlight vs dark, across the world's coastlines?

**Global physical environmental opportunity is the PRIMARY macro level.** Biological behavior becomes a separately replicated outcome, NOT the bottleneck that prevents a worldwide result.

The difference between observed local water-height phases and Earth's solar cycle creates spatially and temporally varying possible windows. Latitude, tidal regime, coastal water-level phase/height, season and the 14.77-day lunar spring/neap modulation together determine when tidal resource windows might occur while it is light. ### Honest physics estimand, no disguised animal outcome

For true original station `s`, native calendar year `y`, UTC time `t` on quality-controlled hourly sea-level relative to THAT SAME original datum/version, define **fixed water-level low-state band** `Q_{s,y,0.25}` = 25th percentile of actually observed quality-valid hours in that station-year (only if original 1-year >=80% hourly coverage and no >72h continuous missing gap). `L_s(t)` is astronomical solar altitude in degrees at the observed original station coordinate+UTC instant (Astral or independent equatorial algorithm, daylight if >+3°, true dark if <-3°, twilight ±3° excluded from binary day-vs-night comparisons; report excluded).

Primary daily `O_{s,d}` = fraction of the **observed low-sea-level gauge hours** that overlap astronomical daylight above+3°, `O = sum_t 1[h(t)<=Q25]1[solar(t)>3°] / sum_t 1[h(t)<=Q25]`, if denominator>=2 real observed low hours. This is physical **light–low-water coincidence**, NOT fraction of actual mudflat habitat accessible or observed benthic foraging. Companion daily `A_{s,d}` = fraction of well-covered observed 24h hours with solar elevation>+3°. Basic mismatch `M=O−A`, which conditions partly on the trivial photoperiod fraction without falsely inferring it as a forced sign of tide×light. Avoid a single day's shared monthly rare-hour sampling.

Temporal/site totals: monthly `E_{s,m}` = low-water daylight-hours / all observed hours with valid tide+solar, plus separate night low-water hours; `O_{s,m}` = proportion low-water hours falling in day; `O_{s,m}−A_{s,m}` is the main co-occurrence effect after astronomical day length baseline. **Use actual 24h quality rules before examining sign** (min 80% hour coverage per candidate month, year 80%, skip unresolvable datums). Independent site-daylength matched spring vs autumn within a station: preselect same annual astronomical daylength within ±10 min at same latitude, opposite solar-year ascending/descending arm, compare actual low-water×daylight window. This can be positive or negative. Include all qualifying pairs, no choosing largest physical pair.

**Hypotheses**: (H1) Co-occurrence varies at the same photoperiod and across coasts due to tide–solar phase and tidal regime; (H2) variability amplitude differs systematically with latitude, tidal diurnal/semidiurnal regime and intertidal flat context rather than sharing one direction universally; (H3) winter-dark/polar daylight and mixed tides generate multiple environmental opportunity states that could impose conditional temporal niche separation; a biological bird/animal response can be tested only in independent coast/taxon GPS with source effort/behavior fields. Never assert any of these is confirmed before calculations.
