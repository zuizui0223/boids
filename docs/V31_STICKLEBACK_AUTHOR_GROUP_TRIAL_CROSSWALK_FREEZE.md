## Independently verified publication facts

MacGregor & Ioannou (2021), Royal Society Open Science DOI https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.210655 used 96 fish randomly assembled into **12 shoals of 8**, repeated across up to 12 trials over 4 weeks, 84 trials actually analysed. Raw original XY source in EIDC DOI https://doi.org/10.5285/08305a43-79b5-432f-ad20-2ddffe0d1077 consists of **84 numbered TXT files**, 25Hz camera, with NA fish-track gaps and LAST frame column. Original [EIDC index](https://catalogue.ceh.ac.uk/datastore/eidchub/08305a43-79b5-432f-ad20-2ddffe0d1077/) has filenames 01.txt through 111.txt with gaps; these names are NOT obviously group IDs. [EIDC __README.html](https://catalogue.ceh.ac.uk/datastore/eidchub/08305a43-79b5-432f-ad20-2ddffe0d1077/__README.html) carries citation/license only, not a confirmed file-to-group map.

The authors separately published [Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.547d7wm8p](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.547d7wm8p) containing exactly **MacGregorIoannou_Data.csv** (41.15KB) and **Readme.csv** (1.46KB) as seen on official dataset page, and the Dryad webpage points to an original source code deposit DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5458081 and https://zenodo.org/records/5490572. Research paper says group ID was random intercept and day/interval source fields existed. They may yield crosswalk, BUT THIS IS NOT PROVEN.

## Discrimination and source independence

A successful source join is a metadata/analysis design advance; **not** a positive ecological result. Prior model I(0.2s) stickleback ~.783/.777 is based on three **files** only and cannot be promoted into an independent-school effect by joining names alone. Future models must ensure no fish group appears in training if held out in test. A common external stimulus could still produce all current>stale predictive observations without fish-fish causality.
