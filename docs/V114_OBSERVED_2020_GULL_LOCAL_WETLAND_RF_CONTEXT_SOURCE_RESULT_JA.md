## Limits and what can be claimed

**Defensible within this one 2020 Zeeland tracking project**: two bird species differ in native RF>=50 *used-point spatial enrichment relative to immediate mapped surroundings*. The difference is descriptive of mapped habitat-class context conditional on original tagged bird GPS/time and mean across four experimental-looking but strictly OBSERVATIONAL solar-tide source cells; original 8 background points are NOT independent animal-selected alternatives. Potential species differences in breeding colonies, coast vs inland use, available waterbody, movement length, and GPS accuracy relative to 30m map cells can generate a contrast. The original RF wetland product is static GIC tidal-flat+marsh+mangrove classifier score, not validated biological accessibility, prey or real feeding. Main v114 result cannot be promoted to time-niche organization, tidal selection, or cross-coast generality. Exploratory many outcomes are correlated, not multiplicity corrected.

**Unproven**: real dynamic accessible habitat \(A(x,t)=1[z(x)>h(x,t)]\), individual reachability and species choices, actual GPS behavior labels, causal tide clock response, internal circadian/circatidal clock. Need independent animal used-available alternatives based on observed movement capacities, habitat classification, and independent coasts/taxa; if based on same data it remains new exploratory rather than confirmatory.
