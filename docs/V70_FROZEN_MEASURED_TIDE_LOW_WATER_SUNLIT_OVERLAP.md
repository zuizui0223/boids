## Scientific limits and future native bathymetry

The true accessible exposed area would require a **2010 contemporary** NAP-elevation raster (z(x)) and sufficiently small same-datum coastal tidal phase gradient (h(x,t)): `A_sun(t)=sum_x area_cell 1[z(x)>h(x,t)] 1[solar(x,t)>0] 1[suitable_habitat]`. Original RWS 2010–11 station heights alone do not identify this area. Dutch historic RWS `WadGrid 2009–2014` and elevation grid archives exist ([Wadden System Report](https://www.systeemrapportage.nl/wadden/data.html)), but corresponding exact-date raster/version, NAP datum, cell grid, quality and public license **are NOT VERIFIED or joined to 2010/2011 GPS here**. Do not substitute a 2023/2024 topography raster while calling it 2010 exposed habitat.

No measured feeding behavior, prey, tagging detection opportunity, independently confirmed GPS recorder UTC offset, tide-causal turning, night-foraging trait, interspecific niche partition or social Reynolds/Boids coefficient is estimated here. **Opportunities at the tide gauge ≠ actual individual accessible mudflat**; all physical and biological conclusions must retain that distinction.
