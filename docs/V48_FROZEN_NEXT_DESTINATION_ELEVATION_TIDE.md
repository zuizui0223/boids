# v48 PRE-OUTCOME — does original Wadden tide phase predict next GPS destination elevation, not an algebraic wet/dry label?

**2026-11-05 JST. Frozen before any v48 destination-elevation-vs-RWS-tide model score is opened.** Previous v32–v44 speed and heading results are known. v46 C-only AHN2 source gate passed for original 2011 Schiermonnikoog (1,018 C endpoints; 13 birds) and Ameland (1,243; 5 birds). v47 B+C historical DTM gate failed (Schier 112, Ameland 373), so **DO NOT claim measured B→C elevation change or front tracking**.

## Ecological question, target independent of tide
Within original GPS three consecutive fixes A,B,C, does the independently measured RWS ebb/flood **at middle time B** improve prediction of the **historical AHN2 first-band candidate terrain elevation z(C)** at the next observed GPS destination, beyond the bird's own previous heading and speed, B position, absolute tide h(B), diel/season clocks? Critical: **z(C)** is read only from the provider's AHN2 DTM and is **NOT defined by the contemporaneous tide**. Do not use `z(C)>h(t)` or `z(C)-h(t)` as primary prediction outcome because h(t) would mechanically enter the definition.

A favorable association is a proxy for tide-conditioned next *destination altitude*, not verified exposed mudflat or prey. The original AHN2 age (2007–2012) may differ locally from year of GPS fix; water/no-data/morphology and unverified WCS 0–1 auxiliary band introduce selection/measurement uncertainty.

## Caveats and explicit no-claims
Original GPS tracks have already supported other speed/direction hypotheses in the same source; v48 is exploratory **new source-derived elevation outcome**, not new independent species/site. Predictor dependence on location/time and source-verified NAP may still proxy routes/commutes. Historical AHN2 DTM is chiefly a ground terrain source with missing underwater cells; usable source C positions are a selected habitat/activity subset. 2010 tile selection depends on 2010 original GPS use, not a random sample of all habitat. No physical `B→C Δz`, actual exposed substrate, dynamic front distance, feeding observation, predator, species niche divergence or social Boids force is measured in this stage.

**Further front-mechanism analysis requires a true local submerged/emerged terrain map and RWS local propagation correction, or a stronger source-quality validation for AHN2 heights.** A positive v48 result only narrows the prediction mechanism toward terrain-associated site choice.
