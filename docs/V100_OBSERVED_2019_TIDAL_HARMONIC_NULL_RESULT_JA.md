### Scientific inference and mandatory claim correction

**The strong v95 F–daily |M| relationship is not an identifiable new ecological law or independent mechanism.** It is reproduced, at least as strongly, by a simple astronomical frequency superposition at the actual original coastal sites using original fitted tide amplitudes and uniform randomized constituent phases, before any organism, food, habitat, light-dependent behavior or social effect is included. This fits the basic physical intuition: a semidiurnal system can experience low water both at night and daytime within a day, whereas a diurnal-tide system may confine the low-water opportunity to one solar phase. F is already a standard tidal regime index; low-water/daylight overlap can be large even when the year-mean signed M cancels.

The original worldwide 2019 **observations remain real** and the M0→M1 held-out-band physically descriptive MSE reduction remains numerically true. But it adds a periodicity descriptor to predict another derivative of the same tidal periods: **under this null result it is not sufficient evidence of biological organization or a surprising new macroecological relationship**. A reader should not infer that high rho, multiple post-hoc sensitivity results or p=0.0002 demonstrates a novel ecological influence.

The constructed surrogate is NOT an exact physical null for original observed seawater: it preserves station-specific four constituent *amplitudes* and astronomical frequencies, but **not** original observed constituent phases, spring-neap phase relationships, storm surges, shallow-water overtides, observed source missing-hour masks or local hydrodynamic cross-station dependence. The 32 physical surrogate correlation distribution is a *model-based expectation* rather than a p-value or proof of mechanistic identity. The absence of F residual cannot establish that all tidal ecology is deterministic; it establishes that **this 2019 estimand cannot distinguish environment-only harmonic geometry from a new ecological effect.**

## What to test instead

1. **Real resource accessibility**, independent of tidal form F: map true coastline intertidal habitat presence and local elevation z(x) on the same vertical datum as h(x,t). Combine tidal water-level model/measurements with accessible prey/roost geometry for day/night. Gauge Q25 alone cannot identify intertidal dry area.
2. **Actual organism time niche**, independent of tide/solar source measurement: activity/foraging/resting labels with effort/detection controls, native individual IDs and original timestamps, tide phases and sun altitude. Test whether niche-phase separation exceeds **a habitat-access-only physical expectation**. This must be independent across multiple taxa and coasts before claiming macroecological generality.
3. **Physical variation not reducible to harmonic form alone**: observed tidal front propagation, local topography, storm anomalies, amplitude×morphology, cross-site resource-window rescue. A null comparing the observed stage window with physically realistic local stage forecasts should respect temporal autocorrelation; do not use naive hourly shuffling as a causal mechanism test.
