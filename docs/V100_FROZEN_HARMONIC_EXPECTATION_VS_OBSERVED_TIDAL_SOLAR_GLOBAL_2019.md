# V100 Frozen before new 4-constituent surrogate responses: test whether v95's strong F–|M| relation is simply expected from tidal-wave arithmetic

2026-11-10 JST. All 2019 original UHSLC results through v96 already known before this document; **post-result mechanism challenge, not first-time confirmation.** v95 observed original 130-cluster F–mean daily absolute solar-coincident low-water deviation Spearman +0.8578; 2019 held-out latitude-band environmental MSE gain ~41.8%. Because F (K1+O1)/(M2+S2) and daily |M| are both functions of precisely the same sea-level frequencies and sunlight clock, this strong association may be **a nearly inevitable consequence of superposing diurnal and semidiurnal tides**, even for synthetic coasts without organisms, realistic geography or food resources. The 2019 p-value tests a statistical association, not biological novelty. This challenge must be confronted before a scientific paper claim.

New pre-outcome surrogate:
1. Reuse EVERY eligible original v94 2019 gauge without selection, same source-frozen 160 IDs and same 100-km geolocation-connected 130 regions.
2. Use source-fitted true original 2019 per-gauge four **harmonic amplitudes** M2, S2, K1, O1 (mm). Generate a physical tide with their ACTUAL astronomical frequencies (12.4206012, 12.0, 23.93447213, 25.81933871 hours) but independent uniform random initial phases for each constituent. No real tide's phase, surge, local within-cycle distortion, missing hours or habitat is preserved. Hence **this is a reduced four-harmonic physics-null, NOT a complete original tide-preserving/permutation null**.
3. Compute genuine Astral 3.2 altitude>3° at 2019 UTC hourly time and original gauge lon/lat. Within each surrogate time series recompute its **own** station-year Q25 and **daily mean |O-A|** on near-complete solar days with >=2 low-water hours, using true solar day UTC+longitude/15 and the identical target mathematical formula as v94, not a direct fit to 2019 observed M. Construct 32 fixed-seed phase replicates per original gauge to avoid adaptive null choice.
4. For each of the 32 replicates, average per-gauge surrogate daily|M| within the exactly same original 100-km groups (observed F and observed M paired to same source groups). Compare **the 130-coast observed Spearman F→mean|M| to the distribution of harmonic-null Spearman**. Report observed relative quantile and descriptive tail proportion; not a frequentist p for ecological causation.
5. Also calculate site-level and coast-level difference \`observed |M| - mean(surrogate |M|)\` and its association with F. A zero residual association means the original strong F law is explainable by the very definition of the two harmonic clocks, not a new ecological interaction. Positive/negative residual must still be viewed as unmodeled meteorology/hydraulics, year/missing-data, not a biological effect.
6. Explicitly handle polar sunrise/sunset as source solar-altitude not daylength requirement. For original 2019 source stations with <95% hourly coverage, distinguish missing-hour bias. Separate four-constituent fidelity subsets (original physical R2>=0.6; original source year gauge coverage>=0.95) without dropping them from primary source census.
7. **Interpret regardless of sign**. If null distribution reproduces F→M, downgrade v95 claim to (a) world observed manifestation of known clock physics, and (b) a new cross-coast environmental-opportunity atlas, not discovery of a new law. If not reproduced, investigate original residual tide phases/hydrodynamic morphology and source geometry with independent data instead of crediting organisms.

No GPS, animal activity, intertidal DEM, food, habitat access, experimental free-running clocks, or ecological niche is part of this surrogate test. The biotic component remains a separate identified-data challenge. Preserve v69/v70 negative time+phase incremental movement forecasts and v86 contradictory endpoint signs.
