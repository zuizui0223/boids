## Why

The sanderling *Calidris alba* WATLAS home-range supplementary 13 (Figshare 26553443) has exactly 6,509 published original localization rows; the red-knot *Calidris canutus* supplementary 14 (26553446) exactly 7,595. Both historical Fig.9 home-range extracts represent **12–19 August 2018** in the western Dutch Wadden Sea (Bijleveld et al. 2022). Their published `tidaltime` values for these home-range selections were **all positive** and previously failed v64 before-high versus after-high labels, but v65 constructed genuine sampled West-Terschelling *published waterlevel* slopes from noninterpolated observations. Published `waterlevel` is not an independently verified raw RWS station gauge at a known clock.

This v66 tests the source measurement hypothesis, **without opening any original next B→C heading or fitting any bird model**.
