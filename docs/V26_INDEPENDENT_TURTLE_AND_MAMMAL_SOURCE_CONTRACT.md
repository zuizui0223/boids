## Primary new wild marine source (not the previously used waterfowl)

Hart, Smith, Burkholder & Roberts (2020), *Integrated step selection analysis of turtle tracking data in the Florida Keys National Marine Sanctuary, 2008–2019*, independent original USGS [DOI 10.5066/P9UZU4GG](https://doi.org/10.5066/P9UZU4GG), ScienceBase [item `5faab074d34eb413d5df1f41`](https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/5faab074d34eb413d5df1f41). The public catalog describes original PIT animal identifiers, step IDs, step length and turn angle, source starting and ending UTC/local date-times, original marine protected area status (unprotected/multi-use/no-take), water depth and chlorophyll concentration. The original study spans the Florida Keys/Gulf coastal waters 2008–2019. CC0/public domain. These are provider descriptions until independently verified in original file bytes.

**Why it may solve a real issue:** integrated step-selection data describe each observed turtle step AND alternative steps from the same original *start* (not generated around the observed endpoint as in the v25 rejected Tennessee source), plus ecological opportunity/risk fields on candidate endpoints. This could permit a field-source conditional habitat/environment test with time-ordered individual histories, if exact original step-ID, time and matched alternative construction are present.

## Secondary global comparison source

Franke et al. (2026), *Local and Landscape-Level Environmental Conditions Drive Habitat Selection Across Terrestrial Mammal Species*, [Global Ecology and Biogeography DOI 10.1111/geb.70212](https://doi.org/10.1111/geb.70212), independently archived source [Zenodo record 15756163](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15756163). The article reports **1,344 GPS tracks / 48 species / 34 countries**, 6-hour original-model step grain, mapped NDVI, ruggedness, human population density and major road distances. This public paper already analyzed comparative habitat preference; this project's **new** inquiry would be prospectively heldout **within-individual temporal history vs environmental availability**, not merely refit authors' coefficients and call them new.
- Check *exact* Zenodo record ID, source license, original file names, compressed and expanded file sizes, MD5, R/CSV contents without reading animal rows. The original authors' supplied step-data archive may be enormous, and may lack original GPS coordinates. Source observation units need verification before any new ecological claim.
- Biological independence: original data consist of existing co-authors' GPS collections; 48 source species labels are not automatically independent experiments/landscapes. Avoid double-counting previous studies or source labels as independent replication.
- Stage A may finish with **source metadata only**, a useful feasibility decision rather than a measured new effect.
