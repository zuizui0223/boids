## Primary temporal fidelity (10s)

Divide both by the training descriptor SD. Temporal loss is mean of six squared differences between standardized simulated and observed lag-change RMS. No pairs cross original windows or generated rollouts; no autocorrelation denominator selection. Compute at 2/5/10s, with lag 25 (5s) reported separately at 10s. Also report centroid displacement RMS at the same lags (native units), all v60 marginal descriptor and position errors, and new observed fraction of fish-time positions outside the SAME training rectangle. Rectangle is not calibrated physical wall. This directly compares persistence/variability, not causal interaction or exact trajectory match.
