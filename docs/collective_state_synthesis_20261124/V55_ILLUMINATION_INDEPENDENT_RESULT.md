## Interpretation and next analysis
This new independently recorded study establishes an illumination-associated difference in conditional velocity-to-position predictive specificity in three-fish schools under the pre-outcome test. Original paper already reports light effects on speed/exploration; new endpoint is incremental out-of-trial predictive value of correct neighbor mapping beyond group mean. Do not say vision alone caused the effect: illumination affects individual activity, spatial arrangement, tracking conditions, other sensory channels remain, shared training folds and small n=19 limit inference. Source-specific predictions are not direct near3/far3 transfer to larger schools. Next test: frozen same-condition versus cross-condition transfer using identical eight-training-trial count, plus common-support stratification by present speed/distance/polarization and day-block sensitivity from documented source metadata. No new causal claim or new numerical result for those proposed tests yet. Draft PR #256; main unchanged.
