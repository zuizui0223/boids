## Common-state standardization (primary new endpoint)
Current/past-only group-level covariates: mean of individual .2s speeds V; mean nearest-one current distance D (native coordinate units); polarization P=norm(mean individual unit .2s velocity), exact zero speed contributes zero vector. No physical calibration. Use pooled eligible group-time median of each of V,D,P across all 19 triads, fixed 2^3 cells (values <= median lower, > upper); this uses heldout covariates but no future target, not a deployment-time threshold. Compute p_ic = within-recording group-time fraction in each cell. Common cells require >=30 group times in EVERY original trial; weight w_c proportional to min_i p_ic among those cells; pre-normalization overlap mass sum min_i p_ic must be >=.20 and >=2 cells admitted, otherwise primary STOP. All 19 trials retained; discarded state mass explicitly reported. For each model/horizon, standardized MSE_i=sum_c w_c MSE_ic, each cell based on the original v55 same-light training ensemble. ## Cross-condition transfer (secondary)
For every heldout real triad, fit models on every 8-trial combination of opposite illumination (9 combos for training bright, 45 for training dark), average losses across these fits. Compare same-light versus other-light on the SAME heldout trial/rows, both full and common-state standardized. Report all B/GLOBAL/NEAR/ROT/STALE losses. Define relative cross-light excess MSE 100*(MSE_other-MSE_same)/MSE_same; also difference of assignment A, and relative extra NEAR vs B transfer cost (cross excess NEAR minus cross excess B), diagnostic only. Do not interpret opposite-condition training as a physical manipulation; feature distribution shift and individual kinematics may explain transfer. No best subset selection, no target-dependent preprocessing. 
Filename date-token sensitivity: only descriptive reaggregation of heldout scores within shared eight-digit recording filename date codes; report counts by light/date code, shared-date contrasts, no claim verified calendar randomization, no independent day-level test, no replacing primary. Whole day holdout with eight same-light training trials may not be possible; explicitly report feasible counts. 
No claim of pure vision mediation: light, behavior, spacing, tracking and concurrent external factors differ. Shared training folds and 19 trials limit inference. Main and earlier drafts unchanged.
