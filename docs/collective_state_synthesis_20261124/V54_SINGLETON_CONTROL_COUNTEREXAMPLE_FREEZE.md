# v54 supplementary pre-outcome counterexample for the independent-single control

Fixed before v54 actual scores and this new synthetic calculation. This is not another empirical test or a model of fish trajectories. Purpose: quantify why independently recorded singleton pseudo-groups do not, on their own, identify social causality.

Three fixed sites, focal site and nearest share one spatial external AR(1) patch field; far site has independent patch field with same parameters. All share one global AR(1) field. v_i=A+Z_patch+E_i; no inter-individual causal edges. Global persistence .8, patch .5, individual .2; global and individual stationary variance 1 each. Two nominal conditions differ ONLY in stationary patch field variance (.5 vs 2), not social interaction, model these as hypothetical conditions X and Y (not actual bright/dark fish). B uses own current and prior velocity, G is mean of other 2 current velocities minus focal, L=(nearest velocity−far velocity)/2, R is L multiplied by an independent equiprobable sign. Exact Gaussian population linear predictions for horizons 1 and 4 with unregularized fit; random sign has zero cross-covariance with all other predictors and targets. Real groups share A and patch; artificial independently recorded singles have identical marginal dynamics but independent A/Z/E for every fish. Compute both real-trained predictive losses on real covariance and the same coefficients applied to independent-single pseudo covariance. Report all model losses, signed assignment advantage 100*(ROT−NEAR)/GLOBAL, and condition Y−X. An oracle baseline containing true present A and focal Z is a check. This does not estimate actual experiment effects and cannot replace v54. Preserve adverse results; no parameter tuning.
