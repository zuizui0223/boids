## Three fixed generators
Unconstrained free rollout (original v61). Rectangle-projected: after each simultaneous .2s position proposal, clamp each coordinate to training min/max rectangle, computed only from all eligible original training fish-time positions. Circle-projected: center = midpoint of same training rectangle, radius = maximum Euclidean distance of ANY of those training positions from that center. It encloses every eligible training position by construction. These two constraints are crude empirical occupancy envelopes, NOT calibrated tank boundaries, not estimates of causal wall following. For circle projection, if proposed position outside, replace it by radial nearest point on circle. No additional reflection, turning force, velocity clipping, coefficient re-estimation or speed adjustment. Preserve exact actual three initial position histories even if outside either envelope; record such starts. Every subsequent prediction uses the corrected position and realized displacement velocity, not uncorrected proposal velocity. Circle may extend beyond rectangle; report both original rectangle and circular occupancy separately. Same fixed envelopes/noise draws/initial states for all paired comparisons. No heldout positions used to define or tune envelopes. No physical calibration claim. Nonfinite or abs(position/velocity)>1e9 native units safety STOP, never use projection to conceal numerical divergence.
