## Scientific question and literature ceiling

**Does adding contemporaneous *within-wetland water-depth heterogeneity*, `Water depth CV`, beyond mean depth and other same-visit habitat variables improve *unseen-property prediction of shorebird versus dabbling-duck use*?** This is a test of guild-dependent community use in habitat-state mosaics, not of animal location transitions, individuals choosing alternative patches, individual state buffering, or competition. The source authors already documented water depth and guild abundance patterns (Hartman et al. 2025, *Ecology and Evolution*, DOI 10.1002/ece3.72032); distinguish prediction transfer from repeating that original association.

## Replication and response — do not retune

- Original sampling row: **one wetland survey**. Experimental group: independent property key = exact `Region | County | Property`; wetland ID = `Region | County | Property | Wetland unit`. The key values never appear in predictors and are never redistributed.
- Prospective source admission: ≥120 valid rows (of 240), ≥50 complete wetland IDs, ≥15 independent property groups, ≥2 original source years, at least 20 valid surveys in each year. If these gates fail, **SCHEMA_STOP**, no coefficient or prediction score.
- Count fields `Number of shorebirds`, `Number of dabbling ducks`, `Number of wading birds`, `Number of secretive marsh birds`, `Number of waterbirds`, `Number of species` must be nonnegative *integers*; source violation is a `SOURCE_DOMAIN_STOP`, no silent rounding. Missing numeric predictor/response or date drops the survey for **both models**, record missingness by original field. No imputation or regrouping based on outcome. Non-missing source counts must all be integers. Native covariate units retained, rescaled using train-only mean/std.
- **Primary response** for a single survey `Y=log(1+Number of shorebirds) − log(1+Number of dabbling ducks)`. This is a descriptive contrast of two source-defined counts, not a disjoint compositional probability or a total count with assumed guild exhaustiveness.
- Secondary responses (separate, no rescue): log(1 + count) for the four source-defined guilds, total waterbird count and species richness. Include all results and negative effects. An original group might overlap another; no additivity assumptions.

## Two matched predictive models and split

- **M0 reference:** continuous fixed original-source covariates: `Wetland area`, `Flooded area`, `Mean water depth`, `Percent of flooded area with emergent veg`, `Percent of flooded area with mud`, `Mean veg height`, `Veg height CV`, `Wetlands within 5 km of wetland boundary`, `Agriculture land within 5 km of wetland boundary`, `Developed land within 5 km of wetland boundary`; plus `sin(2π DOY/365.25)` and `cos(2π DOY/365.25)`. Categorical fixed covariates: `Region`, `Study year`, `Wetland type year of survey`.
- **M1 only adds `Water depth CV`**, measured at the *same survey visit*; all other features and rows identical. Use Ridge regression with fixed `alpha=10`, intercept fitted. Train-only StandardScaler numerical and train-only OneHotEncoder categorical with unseen values ignored. Do not tune alpha on the original labels.
- Hold out **one entire property at a time** (all its wetlands, years, survey visits); train on all other properties. Property-balanced training weights normalized to sum to training-row count. No property/wetland identifier as predictor. Hence evaluation is **new managed property in the original 3 California regions**, not a new year/continent. No independent 2024–2026 future temporal forecast is claimed.
- Score M0 and M1 on same heldout original visits; compute per-property MSE of the primary Y and each secondary transformed response; per-property gain `MSE(M0) − MSE(M1)`. Average over heldout **properties**, not survey records. Report across-property bootstrap 95% (20,000 seeded resamples, `20261115`), median, fraction positive; no within-property visits as independent replication.
- **Primary prospective decision PASS** only if equal-property primary gain >0, lower 95% bootstrap bound >0, and at least 60% of properties improve. Otherwise **FAIL**, not rescued by secondary guilds or by deleting influential properties. Report all original covariate support and missingness. Even a PASS is *predictive* not causal.

## Source/measurement caveats

- Source `Water depth CV` describes variability sampled within the wetland survey area, not the full physically reachable landscape or explicit bird-specific choice sets. No claim of a portfolio-exhaustion threshold, within-individual compensation or a source independent evolutionarily conserved rule.
- Water-depth CV may be correlated with marsh size, shape, vegetation and depth; the matched M0 controls seek incremental *predictive* contribution, not causal estimation. Conditioning on habitat variables may block mechanisms and may mislead causal interpretation.
- Fewer than 2 years or no repeated specific wetland units cannot support true annual state-change prediction. The independently published paper already estimates community response; novelty here is explicit independently held-out **property-level transfer** of depth heterogeneity's incremental value, not rediscovery of shallow-water affinity.
- The publication's field design may include property/region sampling strata and non-independent habitat management. Property blocked scoring is conservative relative to wetland-row folds but not a geographically independent experiment; no claims outside the studied California landscape.
