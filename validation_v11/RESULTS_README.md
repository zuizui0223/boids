# v11 synthetic driver ablation and ecological forecast boundary

- `resource_ablation.py`: 12-seed identified-parameter finite candidate family, species-specific target environment intervention and social on/off matched-noise model
- `environment_ablation.json`: fixed complete summary, 2 categorical grains, candidate-specific information metrics and joint-space total variation, 1200 stochastic-seed bootstrap draws
- `test_resource_ablation.py`, `test_replay_receipt.py`: source equivalence, negative control, normalization, heldout integrity and failure tests
- `replay_receipt.py`: failure-on-any-difference numeric replay auditor; not an additional wild-animal observation
- `plot_ablation.py`: renders two figures from the saved JSON

**This is a synthetic simulation of assigned resource/habitat targets, not competition or a wild species niche law.** The individual targets and species target contrasts are inputs, not estimated causes. v8 frozen empirical partner-prediction failure remains unchanged.
