## P2 publication corrections — resolved

1. **Preserve the sevengill exposure and observation-process definition.** `docs/V134_MULTITAXON_FULLTEXT_AND_CURRENT_RESULT_JA.md:61` currently labels the contrast `干潮／満潮` and the conditional response `受信域に存在した日の検出`. In the original `PMC10565395.xml`, Methods §2.6 (`s2f2`), tide-height classes are defined using the 0.25 and 0.75 quantiles of hourly astronomically predicted height. They are not the discrete low/high tide-event windows used by the lemon-shark paper. The response is hourly acoustic detection within the paper's array-presence days. Its focal paragraph does not explicitly state a minimum daily detection count, so an added numerical threshold should not be asserted without code or another locator. Detection-history-defined presence is not independently established full-calendar occupancy. Suggested wording: `雌の低潮位／高潮位（予測潮位の下位／上位四分位）−.149（SE .041）、雄×低潮位−1.042（.062）` and `検出履歴に基づいて在域と扱われた日に限定した時間別検出という条件付き応答`. Apply the same low/high-height terminology to the derived male point contrast. This preserves the model's estimand and avoids confusing observation with known presence.

2. **Do not call all 118 decision documents original research articles.** `docs/V134_MULTITAXON_FULLTEXT_AND_CURRENT_RESULT_JA.md:7` begins `原著118本`. The saved summary and report's own classification table contain six `REVIEW_OR_CORRECTION` entries, as well as one body-unavailable unresolved document. Suggested replacement: `文献118件の判定を整理し、60件から311件の数値記録を出典に結び付けた。` The title can likewise use `文献判定`. The underlying counts themselves reconcile correctly.

3. **Keep the headline clock conditional.** `docs/V134_MULTITAXON_FULLTEXT_AND_CURRENT_RESULT_JA.md:11` says `正しい潮流時刻`. The raw animal timestamp has no encoded timezone; later §4.1 correctly describes its mapping to author-declared UTC as an interpretation and preserves ±3 h sensitivities. Suggested headline replacement: `主解析で実時刻と解釈した潮流は9通り中3位で…`. This prevents the prominent summary from asserting a clock certainty that the body appropriately declines to claim.
